Conservatoire de Musique

2023
architecture / studio 3
Bath

A place of expression within Bath’s historic centre, drawing on the city’s architectural layers and counterculture music festivals. Concert and jazz halls, rehearsal rooms, workshops and a café bring music education into contact with public life. A sculptural bridge connects the entrance to the atrium, while gardens and colonnades open the building towards the riverside. Musical notation informs the coloured zinc façade; glulam, CLT, hemp insulation and local Bathstone shape the proposed material palette.
